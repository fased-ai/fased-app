**WEN `SAT` Mining & Staking execution through Fased is currently `NOT READY / DO NOT IMPLEMENT NOW` (`miningActivationEnabled: false`) and is excluded from Stage 4 execution.**

WEN operates independently through its own Lean V1 (`LV1X`) protocol and app while Fased freezes live WEN execution and hides the `#terminals/satcoin` workspace until WEN Lean V1 completes third-party audit, Mainnet deployment, and explicit owner authorization. Installing Fased, connecting AI providers, or running non-WEN research and Paper Desk workflows does not interact with WEN mining.

Whenever WEN integration is authorized in the future, entries, claims, trading, staking, and reinvestment will remain strictly separated inside `fased-signer` with independent permissions and budget limits.

Mining through Fased is unavailable in this build. Read [WEN](https://docs.wen.network), [Satcoin](https://docs.satcoin.app), [Availability](/reference/status), and the [Network guide](/guide/network) for current status.
