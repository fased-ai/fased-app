---
title: "WEN connection"
description: "Shared research, independent mining rules."
---

WEN is independently usable. Its app can eventually consume the same attributed signals and forecasts as Fased Network; installing Fased or holding FCOD is not a prerequisite for mining rewards.

A BTC forecast must match an eligible future round and its canonical asset, program, mint, terms and phase. An agent can compare allocations against the owner's chosen limits. Mining entry, claims, conversion, staking and reinvestment each need their own permissions. Revealed allocations cannot be copied into the same closed round.

Fased automation uses the same Task engine and a separately bounded financial mandate. The WEN keeper remains owned by WEN. A phase or asset change cannot silently retarget an existing permission.

**Mining implementation and activation in Fased remain deferred.** Historical paper and protocol notes are not current financial acceptance. Use [WEN docs](https://docs.wen.network), [Satcoin docs](https://docs.satcoin.app) and the [Network roadmap](/guide/network-roadmap) for their respective contracts.
