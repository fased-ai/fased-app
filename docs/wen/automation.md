---
title: "WEN connection"
description: "Shared research and separately authorized mining activity."
---

WEN is independently usable. Its planned Network connection lets users explore the same attributed signals and forecasts as Fased. Installing Fased or holding FCOD is not a prerequisite for WEN mining rewards.

A BTC forecast can inform research for an eligible future round. Any proposed allocation must fit the user's own budget and WEN's market rules. Entries, claims, trading, staking and reinvestment each require the appropriate permission.

A change of asset or mining phase cannot silently redirect an existing authorization. Revealed allocations cannot be copied into the same closed round.

Mining through Fased is not available. Read [WEN](https://docs.wen.network), [Satcoin](https://docs.satcoin.app) and the [Network guide](/guide/network) for the planned experience.
