# Satcoin Mining in Fased

**Mine Satcoin. Compete for BTC.** WEN's primary experience is its own app. Fased adds optional research and bounded strategies under the same rules. This is a planned integration, not live support.

Satcoin is the asset name; WENSAT is its ticker. Participants commit SOL across BTC price ranges in ten-minute rounds. BTC's measured movement determines accuracy. Capital × accuracy determines shares of released fresh BTC and unlocked SAT. More capital increases the share at equal accuracy; it does not change the likelihood of BTC reaching a range.

## Show the complete round

1. BTC context, the five published ranges and separate commitment, reveal and settlement timing.
2. The proposed allocation, total SOL budget, per-round limit, expiry and maximum unsettled exposure.
3. Returned principal, conditional BTC, conditional SAT and execution costs as separate amounts. Include the proposal's effect on its own share.
4. Historical or estimated crowding before sealed commitment; final crowding afterward.
5. Settlement status, pending conversion, actual claims and recovery actions without repeating an uncertain transaction.

Exact-range entries return 99% of principal after the 1% fee to Operating. Incorrect entries deduct 5%–10%, return 90%–95%, and may earn partial fresh rewards. The current forfeiture split is 74% miners, 1% daily competition, 10% stakers, 10% Operating and 5% treasury. Treasury contributions are half BTC and half USDC; bought SAT remains locked without staking or burning. SAT remains limited to 1,000,000 cumulative issuance including 50,000 Genesis, and at most 50,000 mining SAT annually. Accepted capital unlocks the scheduled award up to the 5 SOL threshold, then accuracy and remaining caps constrain release. None of these displays promises profitable resale.

## Bound every strategy

A strategy may allocate to one or several ranges over multiple rounds. It must retain total/day/round limits, skip conditions, expiry and an explicit pause. Pausing stops new discretionary commitments; it does not erase accepted entries or pending recovery. Following a profile is informational. Copying authorizes only reviewed future actions and cannot copy a sealed entry into a round that has already closed.

Claims, staking, selling SAT and reinvestment are separate permissions. Manual, CLI and agent requests must use the same supported typed execution boundary. There is no Fased reward multiplier, privileged allocation, automatic loss-recovery top-up or guarantee of a successful strategy.

**Mine** participates in rounds. **Trade SAT** buys or sells existing tokens. **Stake** receives eligible activity-funded BTC without minting SAT. **Daily competition** ranks the published net round result for wallets/teams, with cooperation allowed. Existing legacy positions retain their own original settlement and recovery rights; they are not silently converted into this model.

Use the [WEN documentation](https://docs.wen.network), [Satcoin documentation](https://docs.satcoin.app) and [existing Fased WEN guide](https://docs.fased.ai/wen) for the corresponding product rules and availability. The new terminal must consume verified protocol bindings before financial actions can be enabled.
